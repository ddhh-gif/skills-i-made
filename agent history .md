---

### Session — YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM

**Directory:**  
**Goal:**  
**Status:** (In Progress / Completed / Blocked)

#### Context

Briefly explain what we were trying to achieve.

#### Actions Taken

- What you did, in clear bullet points
- Important commands, file changes, or decisions

#### Key Decisions

- Any important choices made and why

#### Results / Outcome

- What was achieved
- What files were created or modified

#### Next Steps

- What should be done next (if any)

#### Notes

Any extra observations, problems, or learnings

---
